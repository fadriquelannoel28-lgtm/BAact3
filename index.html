Lipa City Project Proposal

EcoCoins: Recycling Reward System

A proposed web-based recycling points system for selected malls in Lipa City. Encourage proper waste disposal of plastic bottles and cans while earning EcoPoints.

Read Proposal How It Works

About the Project

The EcoCoins initiative is designed to address waste disposal challenges in commercial areas across Lipa City.

Proper Waste Disposal

Encourages shoppers to properly dispose of recyclable materials such as plastic bottles, cans, and other disposable packaging into dedicated kiosks.

EcoPoints Rewards

Users receive EcoPoints based on the quantity and type of items turned in, providing an incentive for active community participation.

Web Platform

A centralized web system allowing users to monitor their EcoPoints balance, track transaction history, and view redemption options.

System Process

A straightforward 4-step workflow for mall patrons.

Step 1
Collect

Gather recyclable plastic bottles and cans before visiting partner malls.

Step 2
Deposit

Bring the materials to designated EcoCoins recycling stations at the mall.

Step 3
Earn

Log in to your EcoCoins account to receive EcoPoints added to your balance.

Step 4
Redeem

Convert accrued points into available rewards and partner merchant incentives.
